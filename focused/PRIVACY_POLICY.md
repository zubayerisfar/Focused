## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account Information:** When you sign in using Google Sign-In or email authentication, we receive your basic account details such as your name, email address, and profile picture provided by your authentication provider.
* **Productivity & Lifestyle Content:** We store the tasks, schedules, reminders, habits, routines, countdown focus timer sessions, notes, and squad/group task collaborations that you create in the app.
* **Social & Friends Data:** If you connect with friends or join productivity squads, your username/display name, streak status, and shared group tasks are visible to members of your group.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Device Information:** We may collect device-specific information such as your device model, operating system version, and general hardware configuration to link and synchronize your devices.
* **Usage & Diagnostics:** To ensure app reliability and service stability, we collect anonymous crash logs and performance diagnostics to help us fix bugs.
* **No External App Monitoring:** Focused is a standalone productivity and focus timer app. We do **not** monitor, record, access, or track your activity in other applications, screen time, or phone usage statistics.

### C. Information Collected by Third Parties
* **Advertising Data:** Focused integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), coarse location, device identifiers, and ad interaction metrics to serve relevant or non-personalized ads.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect for the following legitimate purposes:
1. **Provide and Maintain Services:** To create your account, sync your tasks, habits, and schedules across your devices, and trigger timely local notifications for your task reminders.
2. **Social & Collaboration Features:** To enable group tasks, habit accountability check-ins, and friends leaderboards.
3. **Advertising:** To display advertisements via Google AdMob in free tiers of the application.
4. **App Improvement & Bug Fixing:** To analyze crash reports and optimize app performance.
5. **Security & Protection:** To verify user accounts, prevent fraud, and enforce our terms.

## 5. Data Storage, Security & Retention
* **Cloud Storage:** Your cloud data is securely transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption and stored on Google Cloud Firestore servers with industry-standard security measures.
* **Local Storage:** Certain preferences and cached data are stored securely on your local device.
* **Data Retention:** We retain your account data and productivity content for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be deleted from our active cloud databases.

## 6. Account & Data Deletion Rights
You have full control over your personal data:

* **In-App Account Deletion:** You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data directly inside the App by going to:  
  **Settings &rarr; Session & account actions &rarr; Delete Account**.
* **Request Deletion via Email:** You can also request complete deletion of your account and personal data by emailing us at **isfarjubayer@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request"*. We will process your request within 30 business days.

## 7. Children's Privacy
Focused is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete it from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and are aware that your child has provided personal information, please contact us.
